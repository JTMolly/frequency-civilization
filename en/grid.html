HOW TO READ

How to read the grid

132

Primary × Secondary Root

Rows are Primary Roots, columns are Secondary Roots. The two are never the same, so there are 12 × 11 = 132 combinations. Taking the Root Code Test lights up one of them.

144

Mission Lines

Each Primary Root has 12 Mission Lines, so 12 × 12 = 144. People with the same Primary Root put their energy in different directions. A Root Code is complete once all 12 of its lines have been chosen.

6,336

Full combinations

12 Primary Roots × 11 Secondary Roots × 6 Inner Archetypes × 8 Inner Drives = 6,336 combinations. Finish all three tests in the same browser and your full combination lights up.

The grid only counts people. It never shows personal data.
The 12 Root Codes are a working model that is still being validated. All tests are educational and self-knowledge tools, not medical or psychological diagnoses.
