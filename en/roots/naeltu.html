The 12 Root Codes / Nael'tu
Nael'tu
The Navigator
Lead the way

Naturally sketches direction and vision, so people see a path they hadn't noticed and want to follow it.

TRAITS

You might be Nael'tu if:

  • You tend to see trends and directions earlier than others.
  • You feel strongly responsible for the future, and a voice inside often says, "someone should do something".
  • You're good at sketching the blueprint, and people want to join once they hear it.
  • Sometimes you get so far ahead that you look back and no one is following.

With you around, the team doesn't get lost.

STATES

When steady, and when off balance

The same way of operating is a gift when you're steady and a drain when you're off balance. Knowing which side you're on right now is where using this language begins.

When steady

Your vision is clear and you inspire others, turning ideas into a direction people want to follow.

When off balance

You become controlling and aloof and take on too much. The pressure piles up on you, and the more you lead, the lonelier it gets.

The key shiftMove from leading to igniting: the best leadership helps everyone remember their own direction.
