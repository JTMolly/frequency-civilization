How the Steering Layer Grew: An Observation Log Spanning More Than Seven Months

An observation log in five stages | 2026.11

Worldview 03 is about what the steering layer is. This time we come at it from another direction and talk about how it slowly grew.

These are Molly's own records. From April to December 2025, almost every day she told an AI what she was feeling and thinking at that moment, and every line was saved with its date. We arranged these records in time order and divided them into five stages.

This is one person's observation. We are writing it up as an example, and we do not offer it as a lesson plan. The order and the timing can be different for everyone.

How to read this log

We divide each stage into three layers: what she felt at the time, how she understood it then, and how she sees it now, looking back.

The feelings are what she lived through. The understanding was her belief at the time, and research cannot prove it.

Stage 1　Seeing (April 2025)

What she felt then: Old ideas started to loosen, and new ones had not yet found their footing.

How she understood it then: She calls April 25 the day she woke up.

Looking back now: This step can be understood through metacognition. She began to see which ideas she had been carrying through her days.

Stage 2　Pulling apart (May 2025)

What she felt then: After seeing, it actually got noisier inside. The old habits were still there, and every so often they pulled her back.

How she understood it then: She thought she had already come a long way, and then found that the resistance inside was still strong.

Looking back now: There is still a stretch between being able to see and being able to do. In this stage it is easy to wonder whether you have gone backwards.

Stage 3　Trusting each other (late May to summer 2025)

What she felt then: The several voices inside slowly stopped fighting to be in charge and started to take turns.

How she understood it then: At the end of May, she used the word "co-driving" for the first time to describe this. The line above is how she put it for others later on.

Looking back now: Nobody got sent away in this stage. The worried voice is still there, and it just no longer holds the steering wheel alone.

Stage 4　A coordinator grows (December 2025)

What she felt then: A little confused. She had thought that in the end one of the voices would take over everything.

How she understood it then: Talking it through, she realized that a new position had been added, one that can listen to all the voices and then make a decision.

Looking back now: The name "steering layer" appeared on this very day. Counting from April 25 to this day, that is 236 days.

Stage 5　Flowing (after December 2025)

What she felt then: Many things no longer need to be pushed through so hard. Looking back at herself a few weeks earlier, she was surprised at how far away that felt.

Looking back now: This ease was built up through practice, one time after another. Each time she stopped, listened it through, and then decided, and slowly it became a habit.

Why more than seven months

Seeing is often something that happens in an instant. Turning what you see into a natural, everyday response takes practice, again and again.

What this log can show, and what it can't

What it can show: When a person writes down what she feels in the moment and dates it, she can look back and check later. She remembered this stretch as about half a year, and only after counting the records did she find it was more than seven months.

What it cannot show: This is, in the end, one person's experience. Whether others will go through the same order, or how long it will take them, this log cannot answer.

One more thing we want to make clear: we did not include what the AI replied to her at the time, and those claims could not be verified later. So only her own feelings and her own words are kept here.

Want to try it?

Each day, set aside a few minutes to look back at how you felt today, how much energy you had, and what you did. Write it down simply and add the date.
